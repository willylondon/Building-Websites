Our school

Serving Harbour View since 1977

Donald Quarrie High School is a coeducational secondary school located in Caribbean Terrace, Harbour View. It is named in honour of Jamaican Olympian Donald Quarrie and carries the motto Achievement Through Diligence.

The proposed website gives parents, students, staff, alumni and the community one reliable place for notices, resources and achievements.
